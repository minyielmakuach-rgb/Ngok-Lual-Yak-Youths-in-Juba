About NLYSAUHI

Full Name: Ngok Lual Yak Students' Association at Universities and Higher Institutes
Founded: 2019, Konyokonyo, Juba
Motto: Education is Light
Members: 3000+

History: Started under mango tree with 15 students helping freshers find accommodation. 2021: 500 members. 2023: 1500. 2026: 3000+.

Objectives: Foster unity, promote academic excellence, welfare support, preserve culture, represent members, career guidance, sports.

Values: Respect, hard work, unity, honesty. No tribalism, no politics, only education.

We run academic tutorials, cultural days, sports tournaments, welcome parties, graduation ceremonies, fundraising for needy students, peace campaigns. Office in Konyokonyo, meeting every Sunday. Education is Light because elders without school suffered, we chose light.
Academic Support: past papers bank, group discussions, mentorship, WhatsApp groups per department, future e-library.
Welfare: contribution when sick, accommodation help, insurance far from home.
Culture: language, songs, dances, annual cultural day 1000+ attendees.
Future: scholarship database, online tutoring, alumni network.
